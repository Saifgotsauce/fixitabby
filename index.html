    Questions

    How much will my repair cost?

    It depends on your model and what's broken, so we quote each repair on its own. Fill out the quote form and we'll send you our best price. It's free.

    Is the quote really free?

    Yes. Ask about as many repairs as you want. You only pay if you decide to go ahead.

    How long does a repair take?

    Most repairs are done within a few hours, right where you are. Back glass, water damage and board repairs take longer, and we'll tell you how long when we send your quote.

    Will I lose my photos?

    Screen, battery, port and camera repairs don't touch your data. It's still a good idea to back up to iCloud before any repair.

    My iPhone got wet. What should I do?

    Turn it off, don't charge it, and skip the bag of rice. Send us a quote request right away and pick Water damage. The sooner we open it up, the better.

    Is my iPhone ready yet?

    Text your quote code to and we'll tell you where it's at.

    Do you come to me, or do I come to you?

    Both. For most repairs we come to your home or work and fix it on the spot. Bigger jobs, like back glass that needs hours for the glue to set, are done at the shop: drop it off and we'll text you when it's ready.

    How do I find my model?

    Open Settings, tap General, then About. If your iPhone won't turn on, pick "I'm not sure" on the form and we'll figure it out.
